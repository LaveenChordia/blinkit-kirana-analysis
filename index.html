Research Question

"Is buying everyday products through Blinkit significantly more expensive than purchasing the same or comparable products from local kirana stores?"

This project evaluates:

  • Product-level prices
  • Category-level differences
  • Basket-level prices
  • Convenience/time
  • Statistical significance

Overall Price Comparison

The average difference is only ₹0.65, suggesting that Blinkit does not carry a large overall price premium in this sample.

Product Outcome Analysis

73.3% of the products were either cheaper or equally priced on Blinkit. This does not mean Blinkit is cheaper for every product. Individual product prices vary substantially.

Statistical Analysis (Paired t-test)

H₀: Mean Blinkit price difference = 0
H₁: Mean Blinkit price difference ≠ 0
t: ≈ -0.120
p: 0.905
Conclusion: Fail to reject H₀ at α = 0.05

"Our sample does not provide statistically significant evidence that Blinkit prices are systematically higher or lower than kirana prices."

So, Is Blinkit Expensive?

Not necessarily.

  1. Blinkit is not universally expensive: Average prices were almost identical (₹122.65 vs ₹123.30).
  2. Individual products vary: Some products are considerably cheaper on Blinkit, while others are cheaper at kirana stores.
  3. The basket and convenience matter: Three of four baskets were cheaper on Blinkit, and the convenience-adjusted example remained cheaper after accounting for handling charges and an assumed value of saved time.

Don't ask: "Is Blinkit expensive?"

Ask: "Is MY BASKET cheaper on Blinkit — and how much is my time worth?"

Limitations

  • Sample contains 60 products. Prices can change over time.
  • Product availability and pack sizes/brands can vary, affecting comparisons.
  • Blinkit fees can vary by order, location, and time.
  • The ₹200/hour time value is an assumption. Kirana commute time was not systematically measured.
  • The findings apply to this specific sample and should not be generalized to every Blinkit order or every kirana store.
